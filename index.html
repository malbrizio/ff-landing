Quick Answers

Everything you're probably wondering.

What does it actually cost to get started? +
Nothing. Zero. We don't charge setup fees, art fees, design fees, or contract fees to launch with us. You only pay for what your members preorder — we handle everything before that on our end.
What's the minimum order? +
24 pieces per design. That's it. Most of our gyms hit that in the first 48 hours of their preorder window without blinking.
How long does the whole process take? +
From first call to live store is typically 1–2 weeks depending on design complexity. Once your preorder closes, production + shipping is a flat 2-week turnaround.
How does fulfillment actually work? +
Your choice. Members can order direct-to-door shipping at checkout, or you can opt for a bulk gym-pickup option where we ship everything to your location and you hand it out at the front desk. Whatever fits your community.
What if my members want different designs? +
We actually recommend running one design per drop — it keeps the production batch tight and the delivery date crisp. But we pair that one design with 3–4 garment options so you can hit a wider range of members. A Memorial Day drop might include a men's tee, women's tank, women's crop, and men's tank — same artwork, different silhouettes, one production run, one delivery date.
How do I make money on this? +
You set the retail price on your private preorder store. Our per-unit cost drops as the order grows. Here's a typical tier so you can do the math:
24–36 units$20.99 each
37–72 units$18.99 each
73–144 units$17.99 each
Quick math: Sell 40 shirts at a $31.99 retail price → you're in the 37–72 tier at $18.99 cost → $13 profit per shirt = $520 on a single drop. No upfront cost, no inventory, no risk.
Example pricing shown. Actual tiers vary by garment style and decoration.
What's the Apparel Plan, exactly? +
A free 10–15 minute call where we map out your apparel calendar for the year — typically 4–6 drops timed to your busy seasons. We handle all the follow-ups and reminders. No contract. Adjust or cancel anytime. Gyms on a plan average 3–5× the apparel revenue of gyms running ad-hoc drops.
